HEALTH WARNING

Photosensitive seizures. This game contains flashing lights, rapidly changing colours, strobe-like explosions and fast-moving patterns. A very small percentage of people may experience seizures or blackouts when exposed to such images, even without any history of epilepsy.

Stop playing immediately and consult a doctor if you or anyone watching experiences dizziness, altered vision, eye or muscle twitching, disorientation, involuntary movements or convulsions. Parents should supervise children while they play.

Play safely: sit at a distance from the screen, play in a well-lit room, take a 10–15 minute break every hour and do not play when tired. Motion steering (gyro) and the speed effects can cause motion sickness — switch to touch steering in Settings if you feel unwell. Keep the volume at a comfortable level, especially with headphones.
